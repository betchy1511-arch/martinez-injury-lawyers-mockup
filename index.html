Can I file a claim if I'm undocumented?

Yes. In Colorado, your immigration status does not affect your legal right to file a personal injury or workers' compensation claim. You are entitled to compensation for your injuries regardless of your documentation.

How much does it cost to hire the firm?

Nothing upfront. We work on a contingency fee basis, so we only get paid if we win your case. Colorado contingency fees typically range from 30 to 40% of the final recovery, and we discuss this clearly before you sign anything.

How long do I have to file in Colorado?

For most personal injury cases, Colorado law gives you 2 years from the date of injury. Car accident cases have a 3-year window under C.R.S. § 13-80-101. Do not wait, because evidence disappears and witnesses' memories fade.

What if I was partly at fault?

Colorado follows a "Modified Comparative Negligence" rule under C.R.S. § 13-21-111. If you were less than 50% at fault, you can still recover compensation. It is reduced by your percentage of fault.

How long will my case take?

Most straightforward injury cases in Colorado settle within 6 to 18 months. Complex cases can take longer. We give you a realistic timeline at your consultation.
